Squat Coach

Keep your history safe

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Squat Coach from that icon.

How to use

1. Put the phone on the floor or a bench, side-on to you.

2. Step back until your whole body, head to feet, is in the picture.

3. Tap Start. Stand tall and still until it says "Ready".

4. Squat. After each squat you'll hear one tip.

Record a good rep

Your squats are scored against a good rep of yours.

To choose one: open History, pick a session, and tap Use as my good rep next to a squat or a set you were happy with.
